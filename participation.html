Participation

How teams will join the challenge

This page defines the participation route for the challenge. Detailed submission rules will appear on the track pages.

Who can participate

Academic teams, students, industry groups, and independent researchers. Teams may join one track or both tracks.

Expected submissions

Track outputs or executable runs, a short method description, and optional workshop materials if a team also joins the workshop paper or poster route.

What the site will provide

Task pages, rules, timelines, starter materials, submission templates, evaluation notes, and FAQ updates.

Planned participation routes

  • Challenge-only participation for teams that only wish to join the benchmark and evaluation program.
  • Challenge participation together with a workshop paper or poster, if the team also wants to join the workshop research program.

Evaluation principles

  • Task quality rather than narrow score optimization.
  • Safety and compliance under realistic conditions.
  • Utility retention after safeguards are applied.
  • Clear and reproducible reporting of system design.

Planned public materials

  • Task descriptions and official rules.
  • Data access notes and licensing instructions.
  • Starter kits and baseline packages.
  • Evaluation scripts and scoring notes.
  • Submission templates and FAQ updates.
