Participation

How to Join

This page keeps participation rules separate from the track pages so the general policy can evolve without touching track-specific text.

  1. Home
  2. Participation
Eligibility

Who can join

Academic teams, students, industry groups, and independent researchers. Teams may join one track or both tracks.

Modes

Participation modes

Challenge-only participation or participation combined with workshop paper or poster material, depending on the final challenge policy.

Materials

Public materials

Track pages, rules, submission notes, evaluation explanations, FAQ updates, and contact channels.

Expected submission package

  • System outputs or executable runs, depending on the final track design
  • A short system description for transparency and reproducibility
  • Optional workshop paper or poster material
  • Team and affiliation information for announcements

Evaluation principles

  • Task quality
  • Safety and compliance
  • Utility retention where relevant
  • Robustness to difficult cases
  • Clear system reporting

The proposal currently plans short archival workshop papers of 2 to 4 pages and non-archival participation statements or posters of 1 page.
