Participation

Who can join, what to submit, and how evaluation is framed

This page keeps participation information separate from the track pages. That makes it easier to update general submission and evaluation policy without touching task-specific material.

Who can participate

Academic teams, students, industry groups, and independent researchers. Teams may join one track or both tracks.

Possible participation modes

Challenge-only participation, or challenge participation combined with a workshop paper or poster, depending on the final challenge policy.

Expected public materials

Track pages, rules, submission format, evaluation notes, FAQ updates, and contact information for clarifications.

Expected submission package

  • System outputs or executable runs, depending on the final track design
  • A short method description for transparency and reproducibility
  • Optional workshop materials if the team also joins the workshop program
  • Team and affiliation information for announcements and spotlight selection

Evaluation principles

  • Task quality
  • Safety and compliance
  • Utility retention
  • Robustness to difficult cases
  • Clear system reporting
