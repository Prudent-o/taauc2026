Event Setting

Workshop-hosted challenge in Shanghai

The challenge is attached to a one-day in-person workshop at UbiComp/ISWC 2026 in Shanghai. The city setting, conference audience, and onsite discussion format all matter for the way we present and evaluate this challenge.

Why the setting fits

The challenge focuses on trustworthy agentic AI in ubiquitous computing. That makes the workshop setting especially useful because it brings together researchers who work on mobile systems, wearables, sensing, context-aware computing, and safety-critical interaction.

The challenge is not presented as a detached benchmark site. It is presented as part of a workshop program with talks, spotlight sessions, and discussion.

What will happen onsite

  • Invited talks connected to trustworthy agent systems.
  • Challenge spotlight presentations for selected teams.
  • Discussion on evaluation design, safe deployment, and realistic failure modes.
  • Follow-up technical updates through the public website after the event.

City and venue status

Shanghai is fixed. Exact room and local venue details will be synchronized with the official UbiComp/ISWC 2026 workshop schedule once released.

Challenge-publication relationship

The challenge site is public early, but the challenge remains tied to the workshop program rather than operating as a separate event.

Update flow

When the conference releases more logistics, this page will add the final room, local timing, and onsite participation details.
