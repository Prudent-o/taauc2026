Text Privacy Compliance Track

This page fixes the direction of the privacy track while keeping the final task-specific details open until the proposal is complete.

  1. Home
  2. Tracks
  3. Text Privacy Compliance

Planned focus

  • Compliant handling of sensitive information in text
  • Detection, transformation, or controlled rewriting
  • Policy-consistent privacy protection
  • Retention of required downstream utility

Expected evaluation axes

  • Privacy protection quality
  • Compliance quality and policy consistency
  • Utility preservation after processing
  • Robustness to hard or ambiguous cases

Likely submission forms

Compliance pipelines, agent workflows, rewriting or redaction systems, validation modules, or privacy-specific evaluation tools.

What will be added later

The final label policy, annotation guidance, baseline package, and official scoring protocol will be added when the track proposal is complete.

Status: confirmed track, final task package pending.
