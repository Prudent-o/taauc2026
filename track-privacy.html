Text Privacy Compliance Track

This page is the placeholder entry for the text privacy compliance track. It separates the track into its own space so that policy, annotation, and scoring updates can be published without overloading the homepage.

Planned focus

  • Compliant handling of sensitive information in text
  • Detection, transformation, redaction, or controlled rewriting
  • Policy-consistent privacy protection
  • Retention of required downstream utility

Expected evaluation dimensions

  • Privacy protection quality
  • Compliance quality and policy consistency
  • Utility preservation after processing
  • Robustness to hard, ambiguous, or adversarial cases

Compliance pipelines, redaction or rewriting systems, agent workflows, validation modules, or track-specific evaluation tools.
