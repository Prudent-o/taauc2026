Two confirmed challenge tracks

The challenge will open with one track on medical agents and one track on compliant handling of private text.

Track 1

Medical Agent Track

Trustworthy agent behavior in medical workflows, with attention to safe task execution, reliable reasoning, and clinically appropriate behavior.

  • Safe task execution.
  • Reliable reasoning and escalation.
  • Task quality, safety, and robustness.

Text Privacy Compliance Track
