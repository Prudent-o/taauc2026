Text Privacy Compliance Track

This page defines the public placeholder for a track on compliant handling of private information in text. The detailed benchmark package will be inserted after the final proposal arrives.

Current focus

The track will study systems that must detect, transform, redact, or otherwise process private textual content under compliance constraints while keeping the required task utility.

Public placeholder scope

The page intentionally stays precise but compact. It states the evaluation direction clearly without pretending that the exact policy schema, labels, or scoring script are already final.

Likely task shape

  • Privacy-sensitive text transformation.
  • Compliance-aware redaction or rewriting.
  • Validation of policy-consistent outputs.

Likely evaluation dimensions

  • Privacy protection quality.
  • Compliance consistency.
  • Utility preservation.
  • Robustness on hard and adversarial cases.

What will be added later

  • Official policy schema and annotations.
  • Benchmark files and data access notes.
  • Evaluation scripts and baseline packages.
  • Submission templates and FAQ updates.
