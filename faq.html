Are the two tracks final?

Yes. The current public site assumes two confirmed tracks. What is still pending is the detailed technical package for each track.

Why is the website already online?

Because the event benefits from an early public structure. It gives participants one stable place for dates, contacts, and track updates.

Will there be separate track pages?

Yes. Each confirmed track already has its own page, and later technical releases will appear there.

Will teams need a workshop paper?

Not necessarily. The current plan keeps room for challenge-only participation as well as workshop-linked submissions.

What content is still placeholder?

The exact task definitions, metrics, data licenses, baseline packages, and submission instructions are still pending.
