FAQ

Public questions and current placeholder boundaries

This page separates general public clarifications from track-specific technical details. It can be updated quickly as the challenge develops.

Are the two tracks final?

Yes. The current public site assumes two confirmed tracks. What is still pending is the detailed technical proposal for each track.

Why is the site already online before every task spec is final?

Because it is better to establish a stable public entry point early. That makes later track-specific updates cleaner and reduces homepage crowding.

Will there be separate track pages?

Yes. The site already has a track index page and separate pages for the medical track and the text privacy compliance track.

Will teams need to submit a workshop paper?

Not necessarily. The current plan is to support challenge-only participation as well as challenge participation with a workshop paper or poster.

Will there be prizes or awards?

The site keeps room for awards and certificates. The final policy can be added after the track organizers confirm it.

What is still placeholder content?

The exact track definitions, official metrics, data licenses, baseline packages, and final submission instructions are still pending.
