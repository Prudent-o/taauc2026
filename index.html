Trustworthy Agentic AI Challenge 2026

Shanghai, China · workshop challenge · UbiComp/ISWC 2026

TAAUC 2026 is a workshop-hosted challenge on trustworthy agent systems for ubiquitous computing. The challenge scope is fixed, and the first public release includes two confirmed tracks: a Medical Agent Track and a Text Privacy Compliance Track.

About TAAUC 2026

The challenge is part of the workshop program rather than a separate event. It connects benchmark design, system evaluation, invited talks, and challenge spotlights within one workshop day.

This website keeps one stable public entry point for track updates. Final task packages, submission rules, and evaluation scripts will be added when the track teams complete their releases.

Overview

This challenge studies trustworthy agent behavior in realistic workflows and links the public release to the workshop discussion.

Current Focus

  • Trustworthy agent behavior. We study systems that act, call tools, follow constraints, and adapt to changing task context.
  • Evaluation in realistic workflows. The challenge is built around concrete tasks rather than isolated prompt-only tests.
  • Safety, compliance, and utility. The confirmed tracks focus on reliable behavior under stronger operational requirements.
  • Workshop linkage. Challenge design, invited talks, posters, and spotlights are planned as one connected program.
  • Staged public release. The site is already public, while detailed task packages, baselines, and final submission rules are still being prepared.

Confirmed Tracks

The first public release includes two confirmed tracks. Each track direction is fixed, and detailed task packages will follow on the same website.

Medical Agent Track

This track studies trustworthy agent behavior in medical and health-related workflows. It focuses on safe action, reliable reasoning, protocol compliance, escalation, abstention, and failure handling under higher safety requirements.

Text Privacy Compliance Track

This track studies compliant handling of sensitive information in text. It focuses on privacy protection, policy consistency, controlled transformation, and preservation of required utility after processing.

Participation Notes

  • Academic teams, students, industry groups, and independent researchers may participate.
  • The current plan supports challenge participation with optional workshop-linked paper or poster material.
  • Official rules, submission instructions, and evaluation scripts will be posted with the final track packages.
