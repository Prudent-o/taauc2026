Trustworthy Agentic AI Challenge 2026

Shanghai, China · UbiComp/ISWC 2026 workshops

TAAUC 2026 is a workshop-hosted challenge on trustworthy agent systems in ubiquitous computing. The first public release fixes the challenge scope and confirms two tracks: a Medical Agent Track and a Text Privacy Compliance Track.

Overview

The challenge studies trustworthy agent behavior in realistic workflows and keeps the public release aligned with the workshop discussion.

Agent systems are being integrated into personal devices, mobile services, and smart environments. These systems do not only generate text. They can call tools, operate apps, process sensitive data, and act under task constraints.

This challenge focuses on settings where trustworthy behavior matters at the system level. We emphasize realistic tasks, operational constraints, and evaluations that reflect practical use rather than prompt-only tests.

  • Trustworthy agent behavior under changing task context.
  • Evaluation in concrete workflows with actions and constraints.
  • Safety, compliance, and utility as joint requirements.
  • Workshop integration through invited talks and challenge spotlights.

Confirmed Tracks

The first public release includes two confirmed tracks. Detailed task packages will be released on the same website.

Medical Agent Track

This track studies trustworthy agent behavior in medical and health-related workflows. It focuses on safe action, reliable reasoning, protocol compliance, escalation, abstention, and failure handling under higher safety requirements.

Text Privacy Compliance Track

This track studies compliant handling of sensitive information in text. It focuses on privacy protection, policy consistency, controlled transformation, and preservation of required utility after processing.

Participation
