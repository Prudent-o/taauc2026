About the Challenge

The challenge is organized with our workshop on trustworthy agentic AI in ubiquitous computing. It is not a separate event. The challenge is designed to feed into the workshop discussion, invited talks, spotlight sessions, and awards.

Confirmed Tracks

The challenge currently has two confirmed tracks. Both directions are public now, while the full technical packages are still being prepared.

Track 1

Medical Agent Track

This track focuses on trustworthy agent behavior in medical or health-related workflows. It centers on safe action, reliable reasoning, protocol compliance, and appropriate intervention behavior.

Track page
Track 2

Text Privacy Compliance Track

This track focuses on compliant handling of sensitive information in text. It centers on privacy protection, policy consistency, and preservation of required utility after processing.
