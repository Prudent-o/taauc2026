Overview

The website is public early on purpose. It marks what is already fixed, what is still pending, and where later technical updates will appear.

  1. Home
  2. Overview

Current scope

The challenge is organized with our workshop on trustworthy agentic AI in ubiquitous computing. It already has a public structure, even though the track packages are still being finalized.

Workshop linkage

The challenge is part of the workshop program. It is designed to feed into invited talks, oral spotlight sessions, and final discussion.

Confirmed tracks

The public site currently reflects two confirmed tracks: Medical Agent and Text Privacy Compliance.

Pending material

Detailed task definitions, baselines, data access notes, and official scoring rules will be added when the track teams complete their final packages.

Why the site is already online

Recent workshop-hosted challenge websites work better when the public structure appears early. That gives participants a stable place for dates, organizer contacts, and track release updates. It also prevents the homepage from becoming a single crowded announcement page later.

What changes next

  • Final task statements for both tracks
  • Starter kits and baseline material
  • Official submission instructions
  • Scoring rules and evaluation notes
  • Clarification updates during the challenge period
