Overview

What this challenge is and why the site is already public

This challenge is attached to our workshop on trustworthy agentic AI in ubiquitous computing. The website is intentionally online before the final track proposals are complete, because recent workshop-attached challenge sites usually benefit from an early public landing page with stable structure, organizer contacts, and timeline anchors.

Workshop linkage

The challenge is not a separate event. It is hosted as part of the workshop program and is designed to feed into the workshop spotlight, discussion, and award sessions.

Current confirmed scope

Two tracks are already confirmed: one focused on medical agents and one focused on compliant handling of private information in text.

What is still pending

Task definitions, datasets, baselines, scoring rules, and final submission formats will be inserted once the two track teams finish their proposals.

Design principle

The site is deliberately decomposed into separate pages. The homepage serves as a clean entry point. Each major topic now has its own page. Each track also has its own dedicated page so that later updates do not crowd the homepage or force unrelated sections to change together.

What will be added later

  • Official task pages and data access notes
  • Submission instructions and templates
  • Baseline systems and starter kits
  • Metric definitions and evaluation scripts
  • Leaderboard and award information
Current public position. The website is already suitable for public announcement. It clearly states what is fixed, what is still placeholder, and where later technical updates will go.
