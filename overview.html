Overview

Challenge overview

The challenge focuses on trustworthy agent systems in ubiquitous computing, with attention to safe execution, compliant behavior, and practical evaluation.

Scope

We focus on agents that interact with users, apps, tools, and context-rich environments. The main goal is to evaluate whether these systems can stay useful while meeting safety and compliance requirements.

Format

The challenge is hosted with the workshop and will be presented through invited talks, challenge spotlight sessions, and public track pages that will carry the final task material.

Current structure

  • Two confirmed tracks: Medical Agent and Text Privacy Compliance.
  • Workshop program and invited speakers already listed.
  • Organizer pages and contact channels already public.
  • Track pages will host the task rules, data, baselines, and metrics.
